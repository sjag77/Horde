# HORDE v0.1.0 — "The Third Dimension"

The big one. HORDE is a 3D game now, with a real art pass on every creature and every spell.

## It's 3D

- **Tilted perspective camera** over the arena, a hard key light, real-time shadows and rim
  lighting that picks every creature out of the swarm.
- **Every model is generated at runtime** from scratch — no downloaded assets, so the APK is
  still ~32 MB. One mesh per creature means a couple of hundred enemies still run at 60 fps.
- **Real 3D arena**: stone walls with glowing caps around the edge of the world, a floor that
  recedes into the distance, ground decals for blasts, mines and portals.
- The hero is a **gunship**: hull, swept wings, twin engines with live thrust plumes. It banks
  into turns, pitches when it accelerates, and hovers.

## New enemies — a horde that changes shape

Circles are gone. Every enemy is a modelled creature that walks, leans into its turns and
recoils when you hit it.

| Creature | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Grunt** | The rank and file. Hunched, long-armed, always more of them. |
| **Runner** | From 0:25. Lean sprinter, very fast, low HP. |
| **Hound** | From 1:00. Four-legged beast that runs you down. |
| **Ogre** | From 1:15. Armoured, plated shoulders, hits like a truck. |
| **Splitter** | From 1:50. Pops into a colony of **Mites** — and mites sometimes split again. Bring area damage or drown. |
| **Spitter** | From 2:30. Keeps its distance and lobs acid at you. |

## New abilities

- **Void Vortex** — tears open a singularity that drags the horde into its core and shreds it.
  A real spinning funnel, not a flat circle.
- **Flame Wave** — a crescent of fire sweeps out ahead of you and sets the front rank alight.

Both scale with your items like everything else, and both are in HOW TO PLAY.

## Heroes: innates are part of your build now

Your innate ring **starts small and grows with Wide Reach**, and **Power Rune** makes it bite
harder. It is no longer a fixed aura you ignore.

- **Ember**'s Burning Aura went from 3 dmg/s to **26 dmg/s** — it melts the early waves in two
  ticks, exactly as it should.
- **Venom**'s Toxic Touch: 4 → 7 dmg/s.
- **Napalm**'s ignition: 4 → 9 dmg/s, and **Flame Wave** also lights oiled enemies.

## Boss charge you can actually read

The ground marker is gone. Now the boss **tells you itself**: it rears back, its horns catch
fire, a ring slams shut around it and a spear of light shows the exact line it is about to
charge down. It still commits to that line and will not follow you — dash sideways.

The boss is a full rig: the body turns, the legs walk, the jaw opens when it roars.

## Everything else

- **Dash distance 3.4 → 5.8**, with slightly longer invulnerability. It's an escape now.
- **Bomb mines** are impossible to miss: a spiked 3D mine with a lamp, sitting inside a
  blast-radius ring on the floor that fills up and beats faster as the fuse burns down.
- Spells are objects: steel blades whirl around the ship, bolts fly nose-first with a trail,
  meteors are burning rocks that arc through the air and tumble, Frost Nova throws ice shards,
  XP gems hover and turn.
- **A proper app icon** on your home screen.

Same controls, same 4 bosses then endless, same secret.
